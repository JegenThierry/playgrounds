And I don't just mean the way you look, though you genuinely take my breath away, the kind of beauty that makes a room go quiet, that poets spend entire lifetimes trying to describe. But what's even more extraordinary is what's inside: a kindness so deep and so natural it feels like you were made of it.

You care in a way that is rare and precious. You notice the little things. You remember. You show up, not for applause, not for recognition, but simply because that's who you are. A heart that gives without keeping score is the most beautiful thing in this world, and somehow, impossibly, you have one.

Being around you feels like coming home to a place I didn't even know I was missing. Your warmth is the kind of thing that makes the rest of the world feel softer. Sweeter. You make ordinary Tuesday afternoons feel like the kind of memory I'll hold onto forever, the kind I'll replay in my mind on quiet days just to feel that warmth again.

I don't know what I did to deserve having you in my life. Maybe luck. Maybe fate. Maybe the universe decided to be generous for once. Whatever it is, I'm endlessly grateful. You make me want to be better, not because you demand it, but because someone as extraordinary as you deserves the best version of everyone around her.
